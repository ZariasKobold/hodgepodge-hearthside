## Creation steps
*Run once each. Your words, unchanged.*

> "Well I declare, if it ain't a visitor. Howdy young'un. Welcome to my campsite fire. Take off a load n' come talk to me a while. Many folk know me as the Hodgepodge Emissary but that there's a mouthful. Call me Hank. That there's my faithful donkey, Henrietta. Now why don' you tell me about yerself?"

**C-02 · Starting arsenal**

## Selection order

Three moments, because the app learns things in an order. On arrival it knows
the week number and nothing else — not the result, and not whether anyone got
hurt, since injuries come out of flips partway through.

**1 · Greeting** — on arrival, from week number alone
- First aftermath → **F**
- Two weeks or fewer remaining → **E**
- Otherwise → **S**

**2 · Reaction** — after the player records win or loss
- Won → **W** · Lost → **L** · Draw → nothing

**3 · Injury** — at the flip that lands one
- → **I**

**4 · Archetype step** — reacts to the advancement path, not the archetype
- Opening → **AO** · Bruiser → **AB** · Strategist → **AS**

**5 · Selections** — archetype known, then slot, then source cost vs ceiling
- Opening → **SO** by archetype
- Slot open → **PA** / **PT** / **PB**
- Pick made → **SC** if the source is well under the ceiling, **ST** if at or near it
- Trigger kept → **SG** · Register down → **SX** · All filled → **SD**

**6 · Barter, arrival** — hand size is known, outcome is not
- 3-4 cards → **BR** · 0-1 cards → **BL** · otherwise → **BO**

**7 · Barter, resolved**
- Item acquired → **BA**, or **BX** if it's a top-rating find
- Nothing acquired → **BE**

**8 · Creation complete** → **GO**

**9 · Weekly hire** — scrip known on arrival; the rest per model
- Arrival → **HF** if flush, **HB** if short, otherwise **HO**
- Hire made → **HK** if out of keyword, else **HE** if expensive, else **H1** first of week / **HM** after
- Out of reach → **HN** · Step done → **HD**

**10 · Advancement** — first one → **AD-F**, thereafter → **AD**

**11 · Annihilation**
- Crew member → **AN**
- Leader falls → **AL**, then **MR** if they spend Miraculous Recovery, or **LL** if it's gone

**12 · Healing** — injury count and scrip known
- Arrival → **HZ** none hurt, **HY** four or more, otherwise **HL**
- Injury removed → **HP**, or **HC** if it was their last
- Out of reach → **HA** · Skipped → **HS**

**13 · Campaign end** → **CE**, or **CT** / **CH** on outcome

Hank comments on the barter; he never adjudicates it. No line may suggest a
rating shifted, a flip was nudged, or an item changed hands outside the rules.

All three index by week rather than picking randomly, so week seven always
reads the same. Two players comparing screens shouldn't see different text for
the same week.
